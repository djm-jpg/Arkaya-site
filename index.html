Governance evidence from different platforms, read on one basis.

As AI systems act with more autonomy, the records of how they are governed matter more to risk, price and terms. Arkaya reads those records for boards, insurers, lenders and investors, and reports which claims they support, where evidence is missing and why it matters to your decision. The open schema Arkaya stewards is designed to standardise how records from different platforms are structured, so readers can test them against their own requirements.

The Defensive Playbook

Build the defence alongside the growth plan.

Growth plans explain how AI creates value. The Defensive Playbook does the other half: it is built to make the risk a company carries visible enough for insurers, lenders and buyers to price, rather than exclude it or price it as uncertainty. The decision-maker may still decline the risk or tighten terms. Ongoing advice keeps it current; a Pressure Test or Snapshot is the usual first step.
