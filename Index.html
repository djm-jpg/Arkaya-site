How Arkaya Works
The Custos Methodology

Arkaya delivers its service through Custos — a governance methodology designed in four stages. It is not a compliance framework, a software product or an advisory report. It is a structured, outcomes-defined approach to building a continuously fed governance architecture that the organisation operates within rather than reports against.

The methodology begins with the Governance MOT — a structured diagnostic with every named member of the accountability layer, run concurrently with a review of the organisation's insurance programme. It surfaces the gap between where the organisation believes itself to be and where it actually sits — and what that gap is costing.

Each subsequent stage builds toward the full evidence architecture: obligations mapped to named individuals, quality improvement plans per risk domain, and the continuously maintained governance ledger deployed at every capital event.

The Cost Collapse

The defensive playbook has not been written at scale for one reason: the inputs were too expensive. Major practices have been charging £50–100k for a periodic risk maturity assessment and £200,000 for a static AI governance policy — a document, not an architecture, out of date before it was read.

AI has changed this structurally. The same compression reshaping every knowledge-intensive industry is now operating on the inputs to governance infrastructure. The cost of producing a continuously maintained, obligations-mapped governance architecture is falling at the same rate as every other form of structured analysis that once required expensive specialist time.

The deflation of governance input costs is the mechanism through which insurance moves from overhead to instrument. For the first time, the defensive chapter of the value creation plan is affordable across the portfolio — not only at the largest companies.
