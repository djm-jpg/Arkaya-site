Privacy

Arkaya Risk Limited runs this website. We are registered in England and Wales (company no. 17380022), registered office Oxford House, 15-17 Mount Ephraim Road, Tunbridge Wells, England, TN1 1EN. Questions about your data go to djm@arkayarisk.com.

What we collect and why

When you write to us through a form. Our forms ask for your email address. The call-request form also asks for your name, organisation and question. We use these only to reply, arrange the call or send the brief you asked for. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or taking steps you asked for before a contract.

When you visit. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and does not identify you; we see only totals, such as which pages are read and roughly where from. We use them only to improve the site. It is switched off if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, or if you use the button below.

Hosting. Netlify hosts the site and, like any web server, briefly logs each request, including your IP address, to run and secure it. Our fonts are served from our own site, so no font service sees your visit.

Who handles it

Netlify (hosting and form submissions) and Cloudflare (visit counts) process data for these purposes only. Both are based in the United States; transfers rely on the UK's approved transfer safeguards. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Form submissions: 24 months from our last contact, unless we start working together. Server logs: kept by Netlify for a short period. Visit counts: totals only, with no identifying data.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit it, by writing to djm@arkayarisk.com. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
