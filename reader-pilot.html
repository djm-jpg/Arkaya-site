How it works

  1. You chooseThe requirements, from a list of record types or your own.
  2. You authoriseOne export from one insured, for one workflow.
  3. We readWhat is present and what is missing, with the event rebuilt.
  4. You decideWhether the evidence is enough, and on what terms.

During the policy

The same reading at agreed points, or when the insured changes model, provider or tools.

What it is not

We do not set or recommend your requirements. We give no score or grade. We do not advise on or place insurance.

We take one role per record. If we have designed a record or advised on it, we do not read it for you. If we have only translated a platform's records into the open format, we tell you before you appoint us and have the translation checked independently.

Who sets the requirements?

You do. You choose from a list of record types or add your own. We do not set or recommend your requirements.

Do you score the insured?

No. We report what is present and what is missing against your requirements. Whether that is enough is your decision.

What happens after the pilot?

The same reading can be repeated during the policy or at renewal, against the same requirements.
