# You keep the decision.
You fix the question. We read one export against it and report what is present, missing or not in the export, each line cited. You keep the decision.

## What the reading returns
A reading against your requirements, not ours. Each line says what is present, what is missing, or what the export does not contain, and points to the record behind it.
The event is rebuilt in time order. There is no score and no grade.

## How it works

- You choose: The requirements, from a list of record types or your own.

- You authorise: One export from one insured, for one workflow.

- We read: What is present and what is missing, with the event rebuilt.

- You decide: Whether the evidence is enough, and on what terms.

### During the policy
The same reading at agreed points, or when the insured changes model, provider or tools.

## What it is not

We do not set or recommend your requirements. We give no score or grade. We do not advise on or place insurance.
We take one role per record. If we have designed a record or advised on it, we do not read it for you. If we have only translated a platform's records into the open format, we tell you before you appoint us and have the translation checked independently.
