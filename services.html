Door 1

Insurers, MGAs, lenders and M&A teams (readers)

You set the question. Arkaya reads the records against it and reports what they support, what is missing and what would settle it. You keep the decision and the price.

Appointed Reading: Platform

Standard · fixed scope and price · US$6,000 per platform

You appoint Arkaya and set the requirement: the field sets and weights from your underwriting guidelines. Arkaya reads one platform's published record format, with specimen records where the platform supplies them, and reports to you what that format and those samples can and cannot evidence against your requirement. The platform pays nothing to be read, and your weights are not shown to it unless you direct otherwise.

Appointed Reading: Company

Not yet offered: subject to a licensing check

Once a platform has been read for you, Arkaya reads one company's own records on that platform against the same requirement. You receive a dated findings report on what the records support and what is missing. Your thresholds stay yours and are never disclosed to the company being read.

Portfolio Reading

Not yet offered: subject to a licensing check

Company Readings bought as a block across an MGA book, a delegated-authority population or an investment portfolio. Each company's records are read on their own terms; no aggregate score is issued for the portfolio.

Policy wording and covenant mapping to field sets

Not yet offered: subject to a licensing check · insurer-paid; field sets stay yours

Arkaya maps your policy wording and covenants to the evidence fields a submission must carry, so what a company sends can be read against your own terms. The field sets stay yours and are not shared with companies or platforms.

AI Risk Evidence Brief

A weekly note on what changed, why it matters to your decision and what evidence to ask for. Every item is checked at source before it is printed. Register your interest.

Door 4

Companies

For your own use: what your records support today, and what to record so a control becomes checkable.

Evidence Pressure Test

Standard · fixed scope and price · US$2,500

About a week. One question you choose, such as how vendor models are overseen or how a deployed model is monitored, is read against your own records. You receive a short findings note. Arkaya does not then read that evidence for an insurer, lender or acquirer.

Governance Evidence Snapshot

Bespoke · scoped and quoted after a call

Two to three weeks. A full read of one organisation, scoped by the question it needs answered rather than by system count. You receive a dated findings report and an evidence map you can show to an examiner, reinsurer, board or buyer. For US insurers and MGAs, an edition is built around the programme elements of the NAIC model bulletin. Arkaya does not then read that evidence for an insurer, lender or acquirer.

Evidence Remediation Plan

Bespoke · scoped and quoted after a call

After a Pressure Test or Snapshot. For each gap, what to record as the control operates from now on, and how, so an outside reader can check it. Arkaya does not later read that evidence for anyone else. A checkable record shows a control can be tested, not that it worked. We do not backdate records or present later reconstructions as evidence recorded at the time.

Monthly Evidence Review

Bespoke · scoped and quoted after a call

After a Snapshot. The read is repeated monthly against the same scope and shows what moved: controls newly evidenced, controls whose operation is no longer evidenced, AI systems added. Each month ends in a dated findings report. Today our analysts, supported by AI agents, do the reading. Automated collection of the records is in development. Any change to the review service is agreed with the client; no date is promised.

Defensive Playbook Adviser · Bespoke · scoped and quoted after a call

Defensive Playbook

Bespoke · scoped and quoted after a call

A Defensive Playbook for enterprise value.

Growth plans explain how AI creates value. The Defensive Playbook does the other half: it is built to show which of a company's risks its records can evidence, and where they cannot. Insurers, lenders and acquirers decide what, if anything, follows. It keeps four questions in view: what your AI systems are authorised to do; what the records show about whether the controls ran; where the evidence is weak or missing; and what an insurer, lender or acquirer could use today.

The company-facing services below are its working parts; reader services are under Insurers, MGAs, lenders and M&A teams (readers).

Boundaries

What Arkaya does not do.

Not a decision. Price, terms, capacity and whether the evidence is enough stay with the reader.
Not a governance score, an audit opinion, a continuous-compliance certification, a compliance opinion or legal advice.
Not insurance advice. We do not advise on or place insurance.
Not operational improvement. Changing a control is your work, or your chosen partner's.
One role per record. Where Arkaya has designed evidence or advised on it, it does not read that evidence for anyone else. Where Arkaya has only translated a platform's records into the open format, it reads them for an insurer only after telling the insurer before appointment and having the translation checked, before it is relied on, by a competent checker with no involvement in the work and no interest in the finding. No such checker is in place yet, so Arkaya does not currently read records it has translated.
