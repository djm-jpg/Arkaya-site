How Arkaya governs its own production.

Status

A self-imposed standard, publicly published.

Put simply: Arkaya runs the same governance loop on its own documents that it sells to clients, and shows the evidence on every one.

The Arkaya Production Standard is not a regulatory or industry-recognised framework. It draws on recognised principles, including Boyd's OODA, Beer's Viable System Model and Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety, and applies them to Arkaya's own work.

Readers are invited to test any Arkaya artefact against the Standard. The Standard publishes on whole-integer versions and updates when the discipline behind it changes materially.

The four stages

Arkaya's governance loop, applied to Arkaya.

The Arkaya governance loop has four stages: Protocol, Signal, Human Decision, Evidence. The same loop Arkaya proposes for the corporations and capital-market counterparties we work with governs Arkaya's own production.

Protocol
Every artefact Arkaya issues is produced against the Arkaya Production Standard v1. The Standard names the tools used in drafting, the verification steps performed before delivery, the human approver and their role, and the version under which the artefact issues. Authored by Arkaya, not by the tool.
Signal
Drafting uses Claude (Anthropic) and a defined set of subscription research sources. Live monitoring runs through Google Alerts on the subject terms covered by each document, with primary-source feeds from regulators, exchanges, insurers and brokers. Signal flows continuously. It feeds verification; it does not substitute for it.
Human Decision
Every artefact is approved by a named human before issue. The approver, David McKibbin, Founder of Arkaya Risk, checks the locked formulations, case figures, source citations and construction. Trained human judgment at protocol-defined latency. No artefact issues without approval.
Evidence
Each artefact carries three permanent evidence elements: a numbered reference block with substantive content (not bare citations), a Production Note on the back page recording how it was produced, and an integer version recording iteration history. Read in series, these produce trajectory: time-series proof that Arkaya's production matched its own Standard at each issue.
The scope of AI inside the Standard

AI sits inside the Standard.

AI is one input among several governed by the Standard. The Standard governs production. AI sits inside the Standard; the Standard does not sit inside AI. This distinction matters because Arkaya is positioned as the operating system across every governance discipline, not as a participant in any one of them. The Standard demonstrates the position.

What this is not

Not a disclaimer.

The Standard is not a disclaimer. It is not a compliance footer asserting that AI was used. It does not claim that AI use eliminates error, bias or omission. It claims that production at Arkaya is bounded by a written standard, monitored by named sources, approved by a named human and evidenced in every artefact issued.

Trajectory

Whole-integer versions. Built, not asserted.

The Standard publishes on whole-integer versions. Each version is itself evidenced and dated.

v1, now. The human-readable Production Note on every artefact. Tools, verification, named approver, version, date.

v2, second half of 2026. A cryptographic stamp on each artefact: signed with an Arkaya private key, the SHA-256 hash of the file embedded in the Production Note, verifiable by any reader.

v3, through 2026 and 2027. Adoption of C2PA Content Credentials, the open provenance standard backed by Adobe, Microsoft, Sony, OpenAI, the BBC and Leica. Machine-readable, interoperable provenance on top of the human-readable note.
